Brain Tickler ("the app") is published by Ajay Mathur and is available for iOS and Android.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no crash
reporting. It requests no device permissions and never connects to the internet.

## What stays on your device

Your settings (music, sounds, timer) and your progress (best scores, streaks, and paused
sessions) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. This data may be
included in your device's backup if you have backup turned on: iCloud backup on iOS,
managed by Apple under your Apple Account, or Google backup on Android, managed by Google
under your Google Account. Those backups are not accessible to us. Uninstalling the app
deletes the data.

## Children

The app is designed for children as well as adults and follows the Apple App Store Kids
Category guidelines and the Google Play Families policy. It collects no personal
information from anyone, of any age.

## Third parties

None. The app contains no third-party SDKs.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address and shipped
inside the app under Settings → Credits & privacy.
